Who We Are

A store built on honesty,
quality & personal service.

Established in 1975, Himachal Janta Store has been a part of Totu's everyday life for generations. What began as a humble neighbourhood grocery store under the vision of Shri Baldev Raj Bathla has grown into one of Totu's most trusted destinations for groceries, premium dry fruits, spices, household essentials, and everyday needs.

Today, the legacy continues under the management of Shri Naveen Kumar Bathla, carrying forward the same values of honesty, quality, and personal service that have defined the store since its inception.

Over the years, we've watched Totu and Shimla grow and evolve around us. Children who once accompanied their parents to our store now return with families of their own. These familiar faces and lasting relationships are what make Himachal Janta Store more than just a grocery shop — they make it a part of the community.

For nearly 50 years, our shelves have supplied the kitchens of local families, earning their trust one purchase at a time. Whether you're visiting us for your daily groceries, premium dry fruits, festive essentials, or household products, you'll always find the same warmth, quality, and commitment that have made us a trusted name since 1975.
